Terms of useUpdated 24 Nov 2026
Plain-language summary for the design. The binding text will be drafted by legal counsel.
1 · What we provide

A trade trust score that shows how reliably your business pays suppliers. It’s free for retailers. Suppliers pay to view scores you’ve shared with them.

2 · What the score is not

It isn’t a credit bureau score, a loan offer, or a promise that any supplier will give you credit. Suppliers make their own decisions.

3 · Your part

Give true business details, use only bank accounts you own, and keep your phone and app PIN private.

4 · Sharing is your choice

We show your score to a supplier only after you approve, for the time you choose. You can stop sharing at any time from Trust.

5 · Disagreements

If a payment or detail is wrong, dispute it in the app. If you’re still unhappy, contact our grievance officer from Help & support.

6 · Leaving

You can delete your account at any time from Trust › My data.
